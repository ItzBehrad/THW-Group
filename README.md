🎮 About THW Group

THW Group is an independent Iranian game development group founded by Behrad (724phs) in early 2022.

What started as a creative passion gradually evolved into a journey in game development, with a focus on creating interactive experiences, exploring new ideas, and bringing virtual worlds to life.

Over time, THW Group expanded its focus toward game development, working to turn creative concepts into engaging experiences for players.

Our goal is to keep learning, improving, and creating projects that reflect our creativity and dedication to game development.

🚀 Our Mission

Our mission is to create enjoyable and memorable gaming experiences while continuously exploring new ideas and improving our development skills.

We believe that every great game begins with an idea, and with creativity, patience, and determination, that idea can become something extraordinary.

🕹️ Game Development

Game development is at the heart of THW Group.

We focus on:

- 🎮 Creating interactive gaming experiences
- 🌎 Building immersive virtual worlds
- 💡 Exploring creative game concepts
- 🛠️ Improving gameplay and player experiences
- 🚀 Developing new projects and expanding our creative vision

Every project is an opportunity to learn something new and take another step forward.

📖 Our Story

THW Group began its journey in early 2022, founded by Behrad, also known as 724phs.

In its early days, the group was at the beginning of its creative journey. As time went on, its direction developed toward game creation and the exploration of interactive experiences.

Since then, the goal has remained the same: to transform ideas into games, learn through development, and build something meaningful for players.

THW Group continues to grow through creativity, experimentation, and a passion for making games.
